Fact #2 Watch & Read

The Spartan Scytale

Considered the earliest military transposition cipher device. A strip of leather was wrapped around a baton of fixed diameter; without an identical rod, the text remained illegible.

Fact #3 Watch & Read

The Voynich Manuscript
